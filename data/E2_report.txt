─── LAYERS ───

  L0  strip `;` and `:`              (separators)
  L1  remove redundant parens         ((x)) → x
  L2  canonicalize comparisons        a > b → b < a
  L3  push negations                  !(a < b) → a >= b
  L2' re-canonicalize                 a >= b → b <= a  (fix)
  L4  sort commutative operands       a * b → b * a
